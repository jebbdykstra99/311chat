Terms of use (preview)

311chat is an unofficial non-emergency helper. It links to official city 311 channels. It is not 911, not a city department, and not a place that files reports for you. This page exists so registration can link somewhere honest while the product is still locked.

  • You must be 13 or older to register.
  • Guest browsing does not create an account and cannot post, vote, or delete.
  • If someone is in danger, call 911. Do not use this site for emergencies.
  • A city, ZIP, or the optional location button is used to show a handoff: a listed city's official channel, or for any other US place a local handoff with 311, a search link, and USA.gov. We do not file the report. We do not read a hardware address. Coordinates from the location button are not uploaded.
  • Posts you publish may be public. Do not post other people's private data.
  • This preview does not run an assistant and does not submit your issue to a city API.
