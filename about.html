About 311chat

311chat is an unofficial helper for non-emergency city services. Listed cities show that city's published phone and portal in the location card. The Room Brief stays national until you locate. Any other US place gets a local handoff. We do not file the report. Room number 17. Site id 311chat.

Not a substitute for 911. Emergencies stay on 911. This preview does not file a report for you and does not speak for any city.

Guest can browse. Sign-in is for a neighbor handle and posts. Direct messages stay off in this preview. There is no assistant on the hot path.
